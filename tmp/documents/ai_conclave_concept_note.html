Programme Overview

About the Event

AI Conclave 2026 is a two-day academic, industry and innovation programme focused on the practical use and future potential of Artificial Intelligence in Education, Agriculture and Healthcare.

The programme brings together domain experts, industry professionals, educators, researchers and students through three connected formats: expert panel discussions, hands-on industry workshops and an open student hackathon.

At a glance

Day 1 combines morning panel discussions with afternoon industry workshops. Day 2 is a five-hour student hackathon followed by external evaluation and a closing ceremony.

Purpose

  • Create a common platform for academia, industry and students to examine the role of AI in priority sectors.
  • Expose participants to current industry practice through focused technical workshops.
  • Encourage school and college students to develop practical, socially relevant solutions.
  • Support both technical and non-technical approaches to innovation.
  • Build opportunities for future mentorship, research and institutional collaboration.

Day 2 | Hackathon FormatEntry and Evaluation
Hackathon Format

Entry, Preparation and Evaluation

Open-entry format

There will be no preliminary idea-selection or shortlisting process. All eligible teams that complete registration within the available capacity and comply with the participation rules may enter directly.

Nature of Solutions

  • Technical solutions: software applications, AI models, hardware prototypes, robotics systems, digital platforms or other functional technologies.
  • Non-technical solutions: strategic frameworks, process innovations, policy concepts, service models, awareness systems or other well-researched and feasible proposals.

Innovation is not limited to coding. Any practical and meaningful solution relevant to the selected sector may be presented.

Project Preparation

Participants can also develop or prepare their projects from home before attending the hackathon. During the five-hour on-campus session, teams may present, demonstrate, refine or extend their work.

Each team should clearly explain the problem being addressed, the work completed before the event, the work completed or improved during the event, the originality of the team's contribution and the practical value of the proposed solution.

Evaluation

External evaluators with relevant academic, technical or industry expertise will assess the projects. School and college teams will be evaluated separately.

Suggested CriterionWeight
Problem relevance20%
Innovation and originality20%
Quality and effectiveness of the solution20%
Practical feasibility and scalability15%
Social or sectoral impact15%
Presentation, demonstration and teamwork10%

Technical entries may also be assessed for functionality and implementation quality. Non-technical entries may also be assessed for research quality, strategic clarity and implementation planning.

Evaluation Process and Expected Outcomes

Evaluation Process

  1. Teams complete their final submission by 2:30 PM.
  2. Each team presents or demonstrates its solution before the evaluators.
  3. Evaluators interact with the team and record scores using a common evaluation sheet.
  4. Results are consolidated separately for school and college categories.
  5. The evaluation committee approves the final results.
  6. Winners are announced during the closing ceremony at 5:00 PM.

Expected Event Outcomes

  • Greater awareness of responsible and practical AI adoption.
  • Stronger engagement among academia, industry, government and students.
  • Hands-on exposure to industry technologies and methods.
  • Student-led ideas and prototypes in Education, Agriculture and Healthcare.
  • Opportunities for mentorship, project development and future collaboration.
