Day 2 - Student Hackathon

Date: 16 September 2026

The second day will be an open hackathon for school and college students. Students from external institutions will be allowed to participate.

TimeProgramme
8:30 AMHackathon registration and team verification
9:30 AMHackathon begins
9:30 AM-2:30 PMFive-hour hackathon session
2:30 PM-4:30 PMProject presentation and evaluation
5:00 PMClosing ceremony and announcement of results

Participant categories

  1. School students
  2. College students

School and college teams will be considered separately during evaluation. Each team will have a minimum of two and a maximum of four students.

Hackathon tracks

  1. Education - solutions related to teaching, learning, assessment, accessibility, student support or academic administration.
  2. Agriculture - solutions related to farming, crop management, sustainability, automation, supply chains or farmer support.
  3. Healthcare - solutions related to patient care, diagnosis, public health, accessibility, hospital operations or medical awareness.

Type of solutions accepted

Teams may present either technical or non-technical solutions. Technical entries may include applications, AI models, hardware, robotics or working prototypes. Non-technical entries may include process improvements, service models, strategies, policy ideas or other practical proposals.

No preliminary shortlisting: There will be no idea-selection or shortlisting round before the event. Eligible teams may register and participate directly, subject to the available capacity.

AI CONCLAVE 2026HACKATHON FORMAT

Project Preparation

Students may develop or prepare their projects from home before attending the hackathon. They may present the completed project or continue improving it during the five-hour session.

During the presentation, teams should explain:

  • the problem they have selected;
  • the solution developed by the team;
  • the work completed before the event;
  • the work completed or improved during the event; and
  • the practical value of the proposed solution.

Evaluation

External evaluators will be invited for the project assessment. Evaluation will take place from 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM. Each team will present or demonstrate its work and answer questions from the evaluators.

Technical and non-technical entries will be assessed according to the nature of the solution. The evaluation may consider relevance of the problem, originality, quality of the solution, feasibility, impact and presentation.

Important Points

  • The hackathon is open to both internal and external participants.
  • School and college students will participate under separate categories.
  • Each team must select one track: Education, Agriculture or Healthcare.
  • Both technical and non-technical solutions are permitted.
  • Projects may be prepared at home and presented at the event.
  • No preliminary idea-selection or shortlisting is required.
  • External evaluators will assess the final presentations.
